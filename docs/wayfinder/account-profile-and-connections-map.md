# PactFlow account, profile, and connections map

## Destination

Provide reliable verified-user provisioning, Profile setup, a signed-in People directory, and professional connections without changing Contract authorization.

## Current boundary

- A verified person has one durable User Profile.
- People discovery and connections are opt-in and safety-controlled.
- A connection or Contact can simplify counterparty selection for a Contract Draft, but it never creates a Contract Party or grants Contract access.
- Buyer and Service Provider are responsibilities within one Contract, never account types.
