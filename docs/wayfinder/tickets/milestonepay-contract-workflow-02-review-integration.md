# 02 — Deliver private milestone evidence and activity

**What to build:** An authorised Service Provider can submit private evidence for an active milestone, while both Contract Parties can inspect the protected evidence and its append-only activity history.

**Blocked by:** 01 — Deliver four-step Contract Draft authoring.

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] Only the authorised Service Provider can submit final evidence for the active milestone within its delivery rules; non-parties and ineligible submissions are rejected.
- [ ] Evidence preserves the submitter, time, protected resource metadata, and supported integrity reference without accepting credentials or raw private URLs.
- [ ] Authorised Contract Parties can open a Milestone Review with Evidence and Activity views; activity records submissions and review-relevant events in chronological, immutable order.
- [ ] Evidence and activity data remain scoped to authorised Contract Parties and authorised dispute handling.
